01 ~ About me

Code, design & a camera.

I'm a full-stack developer based in New York, working in Python, Java and the MERN stack. I bridge design and development: I make wireframes, storyboards and prototypes, and I understand the technical limits engineers work within.

Before code, I worked in audio and video technology for large companies like Morgan Stanley, and freelanced in broadcast and as a videographer.

Now I'm moving into AI. I build agents with tools like Claude, and I love working on teams that collaborate, keep learning and try new things.
